REFUND POLICY

Customers can request a refund for eligible orders.

For duplicate payments, customers should provide their transaction ID and order number.

Duplicate charges are reviewed by the payment support team.

Once a duplicate payment is verified, the company initiates the refund according to its payment processing timeline.

Refund status can be checked using the transaction ID.

If a refund has not been received within the expected processing period, the case can be escalated to the payment support team.
